fomc_decision_dates.csv
-----------------------
Decision day of every regularly SCHEDULED FOMC meeting, 2000 through 2027.
Source: federalreserve.gov (historical year pages for 2000-2020, FOMC calendar page for 2021+).
Built by scripts/fetch_fomc_dates.py. Excluded: conference calls, unscheduled meetings,
notation votes and cancelled meetings (not known in advance). 2020 has 7 dates because the
March 2020 scheduled meeting was cancelled and replaced by an unscheduled one.
Future dates (after the end of the price data) are scheduled but not yet held.
